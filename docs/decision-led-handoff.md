# Decision-led product handoff

20 October 2026. This is an implementation and field-learning plan, not a demand or revenue claim.

## Test-site journey

- `/#market-brief`: distributor/procurement lead sees dated observations, possible relevance, unknowns and a next field question before the question box.
- `/briefs/yaba-2026-10-20`: fixed aggregate edition with six evidence cards, denominator caveats and print styling. Never overwrite its archive file with a later refresh; add a reviewed edition instead.
- `/#sample-deliverable`: illustrative baseline → proposed collection → reviewed deliverable. It is not completed research.
- `/#field-request`: decision-led scope enquiry, requested role, geography, category and optional deadline. Requested coverage is not current coverage or a promise. Scenario enquiries retain explicit assumptions; brief enquiries have none.
- `/interviews`: three planned sessions, neutral prompts, explicit local save/export and consent checks. Notes are never cohort evidence or sent to an API. Save only on a trusted device; keep exports out of Git.
- `/operations`: owner workspace for confirmed enquiry, agreed scope/quote/customer approval, editorial completion and queued delivery. Development loopback has a synthetic demonstration; production requires operations authorization. No payment is collected.

## Your three interviews

Use ten minutes for a recent actual decision, ten for unassisted reading, and ten for evidence gaps, purchase approval and next steps. Ask them to explain one observation, one unknown and what they would investigate. Do not lead with a tour or ask only whether they like it.

Record the decision, current alternative, evidence needed, buying owner, objections and a concrete next commitment. Three booked interviews are not completed interviews or validated demand. Compare repeated needs and revise the brief before adding features. A request for a written scope is stronger evidence than praise; neither is revenue until commercial work is agreed and paid.

## Before the first real brief

Assign named owners for intake/scoping, consent, field operations, analysis/editorial review and delivery. Agree a written decision, geography/category, measures, recruitment, permitted recipients, price/currency/payment terms and timing. Budget recruitment, enumerator time, transport, revisit/attrition, cleaning, analysis, review and delivery/support. Derive a quote from real cost and capacity, not an invented website price.

The 115 observations are a provisional initial cohort, not automatically enrolled participants or verified distinct businesses. Explain purpose, effort, voluntary participation, privacy and the aggregate summary participants can receive. Obtain separate return-visit consent and reconcile identities privately. Agree comparable units, instruments and visit windows; document refusals, missingness and loss to follow-up. Publish only reviewed paired results that pass existing gates. Do not draw a forecast to fill an empty chart.

## Activation remains owner-dependent

Configure read-only Google service-account access in the server environment, durable Redis-compatible storage, Resend sender and server secrets; resolve hosting authorization. Do not paste credentials into notes, the public site or Git. Test real opt-in, requested delivery, removal, retries and provider bounce/complaint handling before activation. A local outbox or provider acceptance is not inbox delivery.

The operator attests to customer approval and editorial review; software cannot establish that fieldwork occurred. Synthetic examples must stay labelled and never join the evidence source. No outreach, real email, charge or production deployment was performed for this iteration.

## Next build decision

Prioritize the decision recurring across interviews, then one deliverable customers use in practice. Measure comprehension, scoped enquiries, approved work, delivery effort and a repeat decision. Defer subscriptions, expansion and richer simulation until needs and collection capacity support them. The public brief is the entry point; dependable fieldwork and useful interpretation are the product.
